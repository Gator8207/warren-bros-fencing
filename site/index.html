Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers to the questions Wichita folks ask us most.

How much does a cedar fence cost in Wichita, KS?

Every yard is different — straight runs, slopes, gates, tear-out of an old fence, soil conditions all change the price. For a typical residential 6-foot cedar privacy fence in Wichita, expect somewhere in the $30–$50 per linear foot range, all materials and labor included. Repair work is priced by the section or post.

We come out, walk the property, and give you a written quote on the spot — usually within one business day. Estimates are always free. Call or text (316) 285-9172 to set one up.

Why cedar instead of pine or vinyl?

Cedar has natural oils that resist rot, repel insects, and weather to a beautiful silver-gray over time without needing paint. Pressure-treated pine is cheaper up front but cracks, twists, and rots faster in Kansas weather. Vinyl looks plasticky and gets brittle in winter cold. For a fence that'll still look great a decade from now, cedar is the right wood.

How long does a typical fence install take?

Most residential cedar privacy fence builds (around 150–200 feet) take 2 to 3 days from start to finish, weather permitting. Day one is post-setting; concrete needs to cure overnight. Days two and three are panels, pickets, gate hardware, and cleanup. Larger jobs or wraparound builds can run 4–5 days.

Will my fence stay straight in Kansas clay soil?

That's the whole game. Wichita's heavy clay soil holds water against fence posts, which is why so many fences in town start leaning within 2–3 years. We set posts at 24–28 inches deep in concrete with a gravel base for drainage — not just shoved in dirt. That's how a fence stays square through Kansas winters.

What style of cedar fence do you build?

Standard dog-ear, shadowbox (board-on-board), and flat-top picket — whatever style fits your home and HOA. We also do scalloped tops, lattice toppers, and custom heights up to 8 feet for full privacy. Just tell us the look you want and we'll show you how it'll work on your property.

Do you handle HOA submissions and city permits?

We can help. We'll provide a detailed materials list, fence height, and a sketch of the layout so you can submit to your HOA. For city permits where required (height over 6 feet, corner lots, certain neighborhoods), we'll talk you through what's needed. Most standard residential cedar fences in Wichita don't require a permit, but it's always worth confirming.

Do you offer fence repair, or only new builds?

Both. We do plenty of repair work — replacing leaning or rotted posts, swapping out broken pickets, re-bracing sagging gates, fixing storm-damaged sections. If it's salvageable, we'll save you the cost of a full rebuild. If it's beyond repair, we'll tell you straight.

We launched in 2021 right here in Wichita. Brothers Grant and Alex Warren run it — hands-on, every job. We're newer than some of the big names in town, but the work speaks for itself.
