## How to post (each one, ~2 min)
1. GBP dashboard → click into Warren Bros Fencing → **"Add update" / "Create post."**
2. Post type: **Update.**
3. Paste the caption. **Do NOT type a phone number in the text** — Google can flag it as spam. The CTA button is how people contact you.
4. Add the **CTA button** shown (Learn More → the blog URL, or Get Quote → contact page).
5. Add a **photo** (1200×900 ideal). Reuse your Facebook/Instagram fence photos — don't reinvent.
6. Post. (Posts auto-expire after 6 months, but the engagement signal persists.)

**Cadence:** weekly minimum. Re-purpose the same photo + caption on Facebook/Instagram.
