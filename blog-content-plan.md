# Warren Bros Fencing — Blog Content Plan (long-tail Q&A for AI search)

**Built:** 2026-05-30 from Semrush `phrase_questions` (US database). Volumes are national monthly searches — hyper-local intent is undercounted (Semrush non-localized caveat), but the *questions* are the same; we localize each post with Wichita/Kansas.

**Strategy:** Warren Bros can't outrank 75-year-old fence companies on "best fence company Wichita." It CAN own specific question-shaped queries that AI engines (ChatGPT, AI Overviews, Perplexity) love to cite. Each post = one question, ~800–1,200 words, FAQPage schema at the bottom (same pattern already proven on the service pages). Only target what Warren Bros actually does: cedar/wood privacy fence, install, repair, gate, removal. NOT chain link / vinyl / electric / invisible.

## Next batch — checked against Semrush question data 2026-05-30

Pulled `phrase_questions` for "fence repair" and "fence gate" to validate the next round. Findings:

- **"How much does fence repair cost in Wichita?"** — WORTH IT. Repair's question space is mostly DIY ("how to repair a leaning post," etc.) which we filter out (DIY searchers don't hire), but the **cost** questions are buyer-intent ("how much does it cost to repair a fence" ~140, "how much is fence repair" ~110). Ties to the fence-repair service.
- **"How much does it cost to replace a fence in Wichita?"** — WORTH IT (from the cost pull: ~590/mo). Replacement = high buyer intent, ties to tear-out + new install.
- **Gate — SKIP as a blog topic.** "fence gate" question searches are dominated by **Minecraft** ("how to make a fence gate in Minecraft") and DIY build queries — no real buyer-intent question demand. Gate is better served by the existing service page, not a blog post.
- Lesson reinforced: not every service has good blog-question demand. Filter DIY + irrelevant (Minecraft) intent; chase cost/permit/lifespan-style buyer questions.
