## Q2 — Pricing for typical fence

**Question:**
> How much does a new wood privacy fence cost in Wichita?

**Answer (from owner):**
> A typical cedar privacy fence runs $25–$45 per linear foot installed, depending on height (6 ft vs. 8 ft), gate count, and removal of any existing fence. Send a property line measurement to (316) 285-9172 and we'll send back a precise quote.
