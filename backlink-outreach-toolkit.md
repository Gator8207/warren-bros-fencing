## 4. "Storm-Damage Fence: Your First 48 Hours" — the content carrot

*(Turn this into a clean 1-page PDF with your logo. Genuinely useful — that's why HOAs publish it, and your name + link ride along.)*

**Storm-Damage Fence: Your First 48 Hours**
*From Warren Bros Fencing — Wichita, KS*

1. **Safety first.** Never touch a fence section tangled with a downed power line or a tree that's on a line. Call the utility, keep everyone back, and wait.
2. **Photograph everything before you touch it.** Wide shots and close-ups of all damage — your insurer will want them.
3. **Secure pets and kids.** A breached fence means containment is gone. A temporary stake-and-mesh patch holds things until repair.
4. **Tie down loose sections.** Flapping panels cause more damage and are an injury risk. Secure or remove anything hanging.
5. **Don't haul off debris yet.** Your insurance adjuster may want to see it. Set it aside rather than tossing it.
6. **Call your insurer.** Many homeowner policies cover storm fence damage — get a claim number started early.
7. **Get a repair assessment.** Most storm fence damage is a repair, not a full replacement. A pro can tell you which in a few minutes.
8. **Tree on the fence?** The tree has to come off before the fence is rebuilt — a tree service handles that part first, then the fence gets repaired.

*Questions about storm fence repair in Wichita? Warren Bros Fencing · (316) 285-9172 · warrenbrosfencing.com*
