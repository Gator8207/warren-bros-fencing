## 2. Pricing in the Wichita market

| Tier | $/linear ft installed | Material/post combo |
|---|---|---|
| Budget | $15 – $22 | Pressure-treated pine, wood posts in concrete |
| Mid-market | $22 – $30 | Cedar, wood posts in concrete |
| Premium | $30 – $40 | Cedar, steel posts (PostMaster or similar) |
| Custom / shadowbox / board-on-board | $35 – $50 | Premium + design uplift |

Project totals for a typical residential backyard (150–200 ft, 6 ft tall):
- Budget pine: **$2,500 – $4,500**
- Mid cedar: **$4,500 – $7,500**
- Premium cedar + steel: **$6,500 – $10,000**

Labor alone runs $8 – $13 / linear ft.

> Existing warrenbrosfencing.com search snippets quote "$40 to $50 per foot" — that puts you in the **premium tier**. Worth keeping if backed by quality (cedar + steel posts, well-built gates), but the site should justify it visually and in the FAQ. We'll mirror this in copy: don't post flat prices, but explain the price tiers transparently.

## 5. Wichita-specific quality signals to weave into copy

These come up everywhere in competitor content and customer concerns:

1. **Kansas clay soil rots wood posts** — fix is steel posts (PostMaster), or 36-42" depth wood posts in concrete with gravel base
2. **Frost heave** — posts need to be set below frost line
3. **Heavy winds** — proper post spacing (8 ft max), through-bolts on gates
4. **Sun exposure** — cedar weathers gray over 2-3 years; stain/seal extends life
5. **HOA approval** — many Wichita neighborhoods have HOAs that need plans submitted

A 2-3 paragraph "Why Wichita fences fail (and how we build differently)" section would be a strong SEO + trust play.
